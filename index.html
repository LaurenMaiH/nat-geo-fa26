  • Independent research Interpretability

    Are persona vectors language-invariant?

    I extended Anthropic's persona vectors work to English and Spanish on Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct. Vectors for evil, sycophancy, and hallucination lined up across languages (0.84–0.92 cosine similarity, against a 0.45 cross-trait ceiling), and steering with a vector from the other language cut hallucination by up to 49%.
